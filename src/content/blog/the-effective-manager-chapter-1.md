> People leave managers, not companies.
> — Marcus Buckingham, “First, Break All the Rules”

Becoming a manager again made me want to revisit the latest literature.

There are several management books that come out every year. Management books are [more than a niche market](https://chatgpt.com/share/6a646f5e-ec20-83e8-9250-f13576b7eab2).

So I asked a few colleagues and friends, and got a short list. The Effective Manager wasn’t one of the books mentioned per se, but the authors’ website was, [https://www.manager-tools.com](https://www.manager-tools.com/). They have a podcast, training, templates, and books.

This is a review for one of those books, The Effective Manager. After looking at the site, this books seems to be a distilled version of the podcast and articles. I thought it efficient to read it first.

Instead of reviewing the entire book, I’ve decided that there is enough good material to review it in parts. This portion is just chapter 1, but other posts may review larger portions of the book.

## What is the Responsibility of a Manager

There are two responsibilities of a manager.

- Achieve results

- Retain people

That’s it.

Results are determined by the company, industry, and function of your group. You should know what the results your managers are expecting.

Retaining people is just as important. Losing an employee is a loss of knowledge, skill, and a real person.
